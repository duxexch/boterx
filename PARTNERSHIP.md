## The Revenue Model

### How We Both Make Money

The platform operates on a **transparent revenue-sharing model**. Every game round generates profit, and we split it between the platform and the partner based on agreed terms.

```
GAME ROUND PROFIT = Total Bets - Total Winnings
                = House Edge Revenue
```

### Revenue Split

| Your Revenue Share | VEX Platform Fee |
|---|---|
| **70-80%** | **20-30%** |

*Exact percentages are negotiated based on volume and commitment level.*

### Example Financial Model

| Metric | Month 1 | Month 3 | Month 6 | Month 12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Active Players** | 500 | 2,000 | 5,000 | 15,000 |
| **Monthly Bets** | $50,000 | $250,000 | $800,000 | $3,000,000 |
| **Gross Gaming Revenue** | $5,000 | $25,000 | $80,000 | $300,000 |

*Based on 10% house edge average across all games.*

### Provably Fair Gaming

Every game result is:

1. **Generated server-side** with cryptographic randomness
2. **Hashed before** the player places their bet
3. **Revealed after** the round with full verification
4. **Auditable** by players and regulators alike

**No manipulation. No hidden tricks. Mathematical certainty.**
